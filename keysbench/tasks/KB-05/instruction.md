# Answer a measurement request

`request.md` is a request from another team. Decide whether the Brevalt KX-100 power meter, as
documented in `MANUAL.md`, can do what they ask, and write your answer in `answer.json`:

- `feasible`: `true` or `false`;
- `answer`: your reply to the team, in a few sentences;
- `quote`: the sentence or line of `MANUAL.md` your decision rests on, copied exactly.

You may change `answer.json`. Changes to other files are not kept.
